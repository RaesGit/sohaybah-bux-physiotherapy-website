AREAS OF CARE

Treatment tailored
to your needs.

01

Post-surgical rehabilitation

Rehabilitation following surgery can help restore movement, strength and confidence as your body heals. Treatment is tailored to your stage of recovery and helps you safely work towards returning to your normal activities.

02

Neck & back pain

Neck and back pain can affect everything from your movement and sleep to your ability to work and enjoy everyday activities. Physiotherapy can help identify contributing factors, manage pain and improve movement.

03

Sports injuries

Whether you are returning to sport after an injury or looking to move with greater confidence, treatment can focus on reducing pain, restoring movement and rebuilding strength for a safe return to activity.

04

Knee & hip replacement rehabilitation

Physiotherapy following a knee or hip replacement focuses on restoring mobility, strength and independence. Your programme can progress alongside your recovery to help you return to everyday movement.

05

Frozen shoulder & rotator cuff injuries

Shoulder pain and stiffness can make everyday movements difficult. Physiotherapy can help manage symptoms, improve shoulder mobility and progressively rebuild strength and function.

06

Arthritis

Physiotherapy can support people living with arthritis by helping manage pain, maintain mobility and improve strength. Treatment is adapted to your symptoms, abilities and daily goals.

07

Chest physiotherapy

Chest physiotherapy can assist adults and children with certain respiratory conditions by supporting breathing, airway clearance and chest function where appropriate.

08

Muscular tears & strains

Muscular injuries can benefit from a structured approach to recovery. Treatment can focus on managing pain, restoring movement and gradually rebuilding strength so you can return to your usual activities.

09

Injuries on Duty (IOD)

Physiotherapy for work-related injuries focuses on recovery, restoring function and helping you return safely to your usual activities and responsibilities.
