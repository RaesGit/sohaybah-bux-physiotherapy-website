02 MY APPROACH

YOUR BODY · YOUR GOALS · YOUR JOURNEY

Care that is personal.

Good physiotherapy is about more than treating the area that hurts.

It is about understanding you as a whole person, identifying what may be contributing to your symptoms and creating a personalised treatment plan that works towards your goals.

My approach combines professional knowledge, hands-on physiotherapy care and movement-based rehabilitation to help you feel more capable in your body.

01 Listen

Understanding your concerns, lifestyle and goals.

02 Treat

Creating personalised treatment around what your body needs.

03 Move

Building confidence through purposeful movement and rehabilitation.
